# Meeting Notes — Product Sync, 17 October 2026

Attendees: Lena (PM), Marco (Design), Aisha (Engineering), Tom (Support)

## What we shipped

The mobile app update 2.4 went out on Tuesday. Crash-free sessions rose to 99.2 percent, the best we have ever recorded. The new offline mode is the most praised feature in store reviews this month.

## Problems discussed

Tom reported that support tickets about password resets rose 30 percent after we enabled the new password rules. Many users do not understand that the password now needs a symbol. The error message just says "invalid password", which helps nobody. Aisha will rewrite the error messages to say exactly what is missing, and add a small checklist under the password field. Target: next sprint.

Marco showed the redesigned settings page. Everyone agreed the layout is clearer, but the red delete-account button sits too close to the save button. Marco will move it to a separate danger zone section at the bottom and add a confirmation dialog that asks the user to type DELETE.

## Decisions

We will sunset the legacy reporting view on 1 December. Lena will email customers who still use it, twice, with a migration guide. Engineering will add a banner in the old view starting next week.

We decided to postpone the Android tablet layout work to January. The usage numbers are too small to justify it this quarter, and the team needs the time for the billing migration instead.
